# 0002: Email to inbox for documents

- **Status:** new
- **Area:** Documents
- **Date:** 2026-09-24
- **Mapped to:**

## The idea

Send or forward an email to the app and have its attachments land as documents
in the Inbox folder.

## Examples / references

- Papermerge has an Inbox folder per user/group, where uploads land. This idea
  would feed it from email.
- Similar: Paperless-ngx mail rules, "email to OneDrive/SharePoint" flows.
